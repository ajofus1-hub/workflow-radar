The category is already competitive. Do not publish another generic “best CRM” list. A useful angle is a **workflow-first fit check** for micro and small teams:
1. How many people need access now?
2. Is one simple pipeline enough, or are there multiple sales processes?
3. Must calls, email and chat live inside the CRM?
4. Which existing tools must integrate?
5. What is the real annual cost after seats, taxes, add-ons and onboarding?
6. Can the team export its records if it switches later?

### Freshsales
- Freshworks' official pricing page lists Growth at $9/user/month, Pro at $39 and Enterprise at $59 when billed annually. Growth includes chat, email and phone. The page describes a 21-day trial; it does not present a permanent free plan.
- Official reference: https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/

Prices and included features change. Recheck the vendor pages immediately before publishing a comparison.

## Affiliate programme validation: Zoho

**Fit:** Stronger audience alignment than a generic hosting offer if Workflow Radar develops a real India SMB software-selection audience.

Official terms checked at https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/:
- Standard commission: 15% of qualified first-product revenue for up to 12 months; published tiers show 18% and 20% at higher performance levels.
- Cookie attribution: 90 days.
- Qualification: referral must be a new paid customer, sign up within 90 days of first affiliate click, make a qualified purchase within 90 days of signup, remain a customer for 60 days, and sign up in the same Zoho data centre in which the affiliate is registered.
- No commission on later cross-sells to other Zoho services for the same customer.
- Payout: wire transfer or PayPal are the preferred methods. Request payout at $100 unpaid commissions; Zoho says it takes 15 days after initiating the payment to reach the bank account.
- Joining is free; no minimum sales requirement is published.
- No separate refund window is specified on the affiliate FAQ; the 60-day customer stickiness rule is the published qualification condition.
- Self-referrals are prohibited; disclosure is required.

**India payout caveat:** the public FAQ does not explicitly confirm India-specific bank-transfer/PayPal availability, fees, tax documentation or supported payout currencies for an India-resident affiliate. The signup form asks for country and data centre, and the same-data-centre rule matters. Do not describe this as India-payout verified until Zoho confirms it for the selected India data centre and payment route.

**Owner action required before application:** the signup form requires accepting the Zoho Affiliate Agreement and Terms of Service, and includes a declaration that the applicant is not a Zoho reseller partner/employee/relative and will not participate in another Zoho partnership programme for 12 months. It also asks for owner-specific identity, address, website, traffic, marketing-channel and experience information. No form was submitted and no declaration was made.

## Backup programme checked: Hostinger

Hostinger's official agreement (https://www.hostinger.com/legal/affiliate-program-agreement) and payout help page (https://www.hostinger.com/support/1583263-how-are-affiliate-commissions-paid-at-hostinger/) state:
- Up to 40% for eligible initial purchases of hosting plans of 12 months or longer; no commission for one-month plans, renewals or upgrades.
- Cookie life up to 30 days; a newer affiliate cookie overwrites the prior one.
- A purchase cancelled/refunded within 45 days is not credited; fraud reviews may hold balances for up to 180 days.
- At least 3 approved conversions before payout; minimum $100 via PayPal or $500 via wire transfer; payout requests are monthly and approved payments are released by the 20th, with wire transfers typically taking 3–5 workdays.
- The public terms do not explicitly confirm India-specific bank/PayPal eligibility.
- Applying requires accepting a binding affiliate agreement and supplying accurate account, website, traffic-source, payment and tax information. Do not accept it or submit the application on the owner's behalf.

**Decision:** do not add Hostinger now. Its higher nominal commission is offset by the $500 wire threshold, 3-conversion minimum and longer refund/hold conditions. It is also less directly relevant to the current CRM comparison audience.

## Ready-to-use community answer (no affiliate link)

> If you're choosing a CRM for a small Indian business, I'd start with the sales process rather than the brand.
>
> Write down: (1) number of people who need access, (2) how leads arrive, (3) how many pipelines you actually use, (4) whether calls/email/chat must be inside the CRM, and (5) which tools you need to connect.
>
> For a one-person operation with a simple pipeline, compare Bigin's free tier with its Express plan before paying. For a team of up to three, Zoho CRM's free edition is also worth testing. If reps need chat, email and phone together, compare Freshsales Growth too—but its listed annual price is in USD, so calculate the real INR cost and card/FX charges.
>
> Before migrating, test the same five sample leads in each trial: create a lead, move it through stages, set a follow-up, log a call, and export the record. Also check annual billing, GST, add-ons and data export. The cheapest seat price is not always the cheapest working setup.
>
> If you share team size and whether calls/WhatsApp are central to the workflow, people can narrow the fit without guessing.

**Posting guardrails:** use only in a community where software-selection answers are allowed; read its current self-promotion rules first. Post as a genuinely helpful answer, not a link drop. Do not add Workflow Radar or affiliate links unless the community permits them and the relationship is clearly disclosed. Do not reuse the same text across multiple communities.
