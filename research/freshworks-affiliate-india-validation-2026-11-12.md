# Freshworks affiliate programme: India payout and terms validation

**Reviewed:** 12 November 2026  
**Decision:** Watchlist only — do not apply or publish a Freshworks affiliate CTA yet.  
**Scope:** Freshdesk and Freshservice as possible B2B support / IT-service software offers for Workflow Radar.

## Executive finding

Freshworks is a potentially relevant second B2B software offer, but the current official agreement is materially different from the still-indexed FAQ. The agreement effective 31 March 2026 is controlling for the published commission tiers and eligible products. The FAQ still states an older 15% rate and a 90-day cookie. Do not copy the stale FAQ commission into a comparison or promise a 90-day cookie until Freshworks confirms that the FAQ's attribution statement remains valid under the current agreement.

No application was submitted. Signing up itself represents that the applicant has read, understood and agreed to a binding agreement, is of legal age, and has authority to enter it. That is an owner-only action.

## Current official programme terms

Source: [Freshworks Affiliate Partner Agreement, effective 31 March 2026](https://www.freshworks.com/company/affiliate-partner/terms-and-conditions/).

- **Eligible products:** Freshdesk and Freshservice.
- **Referral method:** use the programme referral URL. Freshworks decides whether a referral is qualified.
- **Conversion window:** the qualified referral must become a paying customer within three months after referral submission, with a subscription term of at least one month. The parties may agree in writing to extend or waive the window.
- **Base tier:** fewer than 50 qualified referrals that become paying customers in a month — 20% of the customer's first-year subscription revenue, starting from the first paid month.
- **Power tier:** 50–100 such customers in a month — 25%.
- **Affiliate Champions:** 101 or more in a month — 30%.
- **Commission duration:** up to 12 months from the first paid month; stops earlier if the customer terminates.
- **No upgrade uplift:** later MRR increases, upsells and additional purchases do not generate extra commission.
- **Attribution claims:** must be submitted in the same financial year as the sale/conversion.
- **Payout timing:** Freshworks calculates monthly and pays through PartnerStack's regular payment schedule. The Freshworks agreement does not state a programme-specific minimum withdrawal threshold or exact cash-arrival date.
- **Refund / reversal exposure:** Freshworks may recover commissions paid in error, if a customer fails to make first-year subscription payments within 60 days of the due date, or if the customer contract ends early. It may offset future commissions or invoice the affiliate; an invoice is due within 30 days.

## Important conflict: current agreement vs FAQ

Source: [Freshworks Affiliate FAQ](https://www.freshworks.com/company/affiliate-partner/faq/).

The FAQ still says:
- 15% of monthly recurring revenue for the first 12 months;
- 90-day cookie attribution;
- payout before the end of the following month;
- PayPal or Stripe as the withdrawal provider;
- affiliate approval after application review for all products in the programme.

The newer agreement (effective 31 March 2026) says 20% / 25% / 30% tiers and names Freshdesk + Freshservice. Its three-month conversion period is not, by itself, proof of a browser cookie's technical duration. Treat the FAQ's 90-day cookie and 15% figure as **unconfirmed / potentially stale** until Freshworks confirms in writing which FAQ statements remain current. Do not advertise the two sources as if they agree.

## India payout assessment

### PartnerStack generic rules

Official PartnerStack documentation:
- [How do I get paid?](https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009501113-How-do-I-get-paid)
- [Configuring payout providers](https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009377934-Configuring-payout-providers)

PartnerStack's general withdrawal minimum is **more than US$5 in approved, available commissions**. Commissions are first reviewed by the programme company; they are not cash while pending. The normal PartnerStack cycle makes approved and company-funded commissions available around the 13th of the following month, but company-specific schedules and delays apply.

PartnerStack's general payout guide lists:
- PayPal (PartnerStack processing fee: 2%, capped at US$20);
- Stripe (US$2.25 + 0.25%, capped at US$20);
- direct deposit through Airwallex in India (local transfer fee schedule applies; for India, US$4 + 1% for withdrawals below US$1,000, with lower percentage tiers at larger amounts).

However, the Freshworks FAQ specifically names **PayPal or Stripe**. Do not assume that the generic PartnerStack Airwallex direct-deposit option is enabled for the Freshworks programme without confirmation from Freshworks / PartnerStack.

### PayPal India

Official PayPal India material:
- [Receiving payments / KYC](https://www.paypal.com/in/cshelp/article/how-do-i-receive-payments-through-paypal-help667)
- [Automatic bank withdrawals](https://www.paypal.com/in/cshelp/article/how-do-i-transfer-funds-to-my-bank-account-help394)
- [PayPal India business fees](https://www.paypal.com/in/business/paypal-business-fees)

PayPal says Indian recipients need verified PAN, a linked Indian bank account, a purpose code, business-nature details and identity checks to receive international payments. Funds are automatically transferred to the preferred local bank account, normally within up to five business days. The published India merchant fee for receiving international commercial transactions is 4.40% + a fixed fee; currency conversion can add a further conversion margin.

This makes PayPal a plausible India route **if** Freshworks/PartnerStack accepts the owner's eligible PayPal account for this programme. The owner must verify their own account type, KYC, purpose code and receiving capability. None of those settings were accessed or changed.

### Stripe India

Official Stripe material says new Indian Stripe accounts are invite-only. For international payments, Stripe says an Indian account must be active and verified, and individuals cannot accept international payments; the account must be a registered company, LLC or sole proprietorship meeting Stripe's requirements. Therefore Stripe is not treated as a dependable payout route for this owner unless their existing account status independently qualifies.

## Unit economics (formula only; no assumed plan price)

At the current base tier:

**Gross first-year commission = eligible first-year subscription revenue × 20%**

The 25% and 30% tiers require 50–100 or 101+ qualified new paying customers in the same month, respectively. These are high thresholds and should not be used in a forecast. Actual eligible revenue is subject to subscription duration, invoice realization, cancellations, refunds, reversals and PartnerStack / payout-provider fees.

No AOV, conversion rate, traffic, RPM or expected revenue has been invented.

## Audience and distribution fit

Potential buyer intent to validate before building a page:
- Freshdesk vs Zoho Desk for small Indian support teams;
- Freshservice pricing and alternatives for IT teams;
- helpdesk software for small businesses;
- support ticketing tools with mobile-agent workflows.

These are **query hypotheses**, not measured search volumes. The current Workflow Radar asset is a CRM pricing calculator; it does not prove a helpdesk audience exists. Before adding a page or CTA, validate Search Console queries (once the owner property is connected), current Indian pricing, product limitations, mobile workflows, competitor pages, and whether the audience actually includes support / IT decision-makers.

A separate Freshworks page should compare suitable alternatives honestly and disclose the relationship clearly. Do not recommend Freshdesk or Freshservice when a simpler or lower-cost alternative better fits the buyer.

## Application and owner action

The programme is free to apply to, but the current agreement explicitly makes signup an acceptance of binding terms and requires applicant representations about legal age and authority. Therefore:

- **Not applied:** no account creation, checkbox acceptance, declaration or signature was made.
- **Owner action required before application:** review and personally accept the 31 March 2026 agreement; verify the applicant's legal capacity and authority; choose an available India-compatible payout route; complete any provider KYC/tax details.
- Ask Freshworks to confirm in writing: (1) whether the 90-day cookie statement remains current under the March 2026 agreement, (2) whether the programme permits Airwallex direct deposit to an Indian bank despite the FAQ naming PayPal/Stripe, (3) whether there is any Freshworks-specific hold / minimum beyond PartnerStack's general US$5 withdrawal rule, and (4) whether the listed tier thresholds are evaluated on the calendar month.
