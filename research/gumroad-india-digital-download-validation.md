# India-ready digital download: CRM Fit & Total-Cost Scorecard

**Research date:** 2026-11-12  
**Status:** Draft asset created locally; not published, priced, or listed. No storefront was created and no account settings were changed.

## Decision

A small, practical spreadsheet can complement Workflow Radar's existing software-comparison audience. The prepared workbook, `workflow-radar-crm-fit-scorecard.xlsx`, lets a small team compare up to five CRMs by first-year cost and workflow fit. It includes:
- editable inputs for users, seat price, add-ons, setup, tax and FX/card costs;
- formulas for estimated first-year and per-user monthly cost;
- weighted scores for ease, automation, integrations, mobile workflow, reporting and exportability;
- a consistent five-dummy-lead trial checklist;
- reminders to verify current vendor prices and avoid entering personal/customer data.

This is a **supporting buyer tool**, not evidence of a proven paid-download market. Keep it free as a useful lead magnet first; only consider a paid expanded edition if downloads, repeat use, feedback and opt-in interest demonstrate demand. Do not create another storefront.

## India payout verification: Gumroad

Official Gumroad help confirms India is supported for local bank payouts in INR:
- India appears in the supported bank-payout country list with payout currency INR.
- India's listed payout day is Tuesday.
- Standard minimum payout is $100 USD; the help page lists higher country-specific minimums only where explicitly shown, and India has no extra minimum displayed.
- Weekly/monthly/quarterly payouts include sales through the previous Friday UTC and impose a minimum 7-day balance hold.
- Bank transfers can take 2–7 business days.
- New-account review can take 1–3 weeks; Gumroad says it typically looks for 3–4 sales with a balance over $100, though every account is reviewed individually.
- Identity checks may require government photo ID, proof of residence, and other country-specific information.

Official source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid  
Payout settings/identity source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/260-your-payout-settings-page  
Payout delay source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/281-payout-delays

Important distinction: Gumroad says buyer checkout can support local methods such as UPI in India, but that is separate from creator payouts. PayPal Connect for checkout is not available to creators in India. The verified seller route relevant here is local bank payout in INR, subject to the owner's own identity and bank verification. No payout settings were inspected or changed.

## Fees and illustrative unit economics

Official pricing: https://gumroad.com/pricing  
Official fee details: https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees

- Direct/profile/link sales: 10% + $0.50 per transaction, plus card processing (listed by Gumroad as 2.9% + $0.30 for card payments).
- Gumroad Discover marketplace sales: 30% flat, including processing.
- No monthly fee.

Illustrative USD net before any applicable taxes/adjustments, refunds, currency conversion or other payment-method differences:

| Price | Direct-link estimated net | Discover estimated net |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $5 | $3.56 | $3.50 |
| $9 | $7.04 | $6.30 |
| $15 | $12.27 | $10.50 |

These are arithmetic estimates from published fee rates, not actual account quotes or guaranteed proceeds. The fixed $0.50 and $0.30 charges make very low-priced downloads less attractive. A $9–$15 expanded product is only a hypothesis; do not set a price before testing usefulness and buyer response.

## Distribution and monetisation test

1. First offer the scorecard as a free, direct download from the existing Workflow Radar comparison content only after the owner confirms the file and page placement.
2. Do not require an email address to download it. If an opt-in is later added, it must be explicit, optional and privacy-respecting.
3. Share one genuinely useful explanation of how to compare first-year CRM cost in a community only if its current rules allow it; do not mass-post or drop links.
4. Measure unique qualified visits, downloads, repeat use/feedback, opt-ins (if explicitly enabled), and outbound clicks separately.
5. Do not list a paid version until evidence shows repeated use or explicit requests for an expanded template. If tested later, use the owner's existing Gumroad account/store only.
