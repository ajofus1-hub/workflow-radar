### Critical due-diligence limits

- The public lab onboarding page asks for lab name, member GitHub logins and skills; no ID/KYC field is shown. Solo-lab eligibility is not explicitly stated; later verification is unknown.
- The buyer-company onboarding page asks legal name, GSTIN, incorporation certificate and GST registration. This is for buyers, not labs.
- No public terms, privacy policy, payout policy, escrow provider, fee schedule, dispute process, tax treatment, legal entity or contact page was found. Common /terms, /privacy, /payout, /verification, /faq, /about and /contact routes returned 404. Site identifies itself only as “A NORTH PRODUCT”.
- Homepage says ₹ releases same day, but no payout rail is specified. UPI appears in M-2419's task title/mock service, not as a documented winner payout method.
- Buyer identity: Zenlark Payments not independently corroborated; Kirana Cloud matches the public [kirana.cloud](https://kirana.cloud/) brand/site, but no BountBee relationship or legal-entity connection is established; Meridian Health is non-unique and no exact linkage is established.
- Escrow-captured dates are BountBee timeline statements, not independent transaction proof. No escrow provider or transaction reference is published.

**Decision:** conditional hold only; do not count any mission as eligible or proposal-ready. Do not create a lab, claim a slot or accept terms without owner approval. Require written clarification of India eligibility, solo-lab rules, payout rail, any KYC/ID checks, legal operator, buyer identities, fees/taxes and dispute handling.
