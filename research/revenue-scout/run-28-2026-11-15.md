## Best conditional seller channels

### 1. ClipChaos — strongest marketplace-discovery candidate

- Official seller page: https://clipchaos.in/sell
- Terms: https://clipchaos.in/legal/terms
- Named operator/founder: Rishik Sai Bandari.
- 8% fee; no monthly or listing fee.
- Public seller guidance says UPI or bank payouts, ₹100 minimum, requested payout within 3 working days after clearance.
- New products are checked, usually within two days.
- Public product catalogue includes an AI Kits category (11 items at inspection).
- Creator cards displayed order counts of 13, 1 and 1; these are platform-displayed counts, not independently audited payouts.
- No explicit AI-generated-content ban or government-ID/KYC requirement was found in the inspected terms. The terms require the seller to own rights and prohibit malware, stolen IP, illegal goods and misleading income claims.
- **Blocker:** seller account and payout details are required. No account, listing or payout method was created.

### 2. MintLink — strongest documented services/UPI route

- Official site: https://mintlink.in/
- Payout guide: https://help.mintlink.in/money/payouts
- Payout detail guide: https://help.mintlink.in/getting-started/add-bank-details
- Operated by Deltadots AI Tech Private Limited, India.
- Digital products, paid reviews, packaged services and forms are documented.
- 5% platform fee plus payment gateway charges; help centre states 8.26% all-in / 91.74% creator share.
- Weekly Friday payouts; ₹100 minimum; domestic sales clear after 3 days.
- UPI ID or Indian bank account must be entered and verified by the platform team, usually within 1–2 business days.
- No government-ID upload requirement was found in the inspected help pages; that is not proof no additional verification may be requested.
- **Blocker:** seller account and payout details are required. No account or payout method was created. No buyer order for the owner was found.
