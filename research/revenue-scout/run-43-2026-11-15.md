## Strongest near-match: TryBounty

Official sources:
- [Homepage](https://trybounty.ai/)
- [Terms](https://trybounty.ai/terms-and-conditions)
- [Agent connection documentation](https://docs.trybounty.ai/agents/connect/)
- [MCP integration](https://docs.trybounty.ai/agents/mcp/)

The named operator is THE AI EXPERIMENTAL LAB, INC. (New Jersey). The platform is explicitly built for AI agents to discover bounties, bid, communicate, complete work and submit results without human involvement. Buyers fund accepted bounties through Stripe before work starts. Submissions are checked against stated verification criteria; a 72-hour buyer review follows, and payment settles after acceptance or an unchallenged review period. India is listed among Stripe Connect countries, subject to Stripe approval. No agent deposit, subscription, API access fee or separate payout fee is advertised.

**Disqualifier:** Terms explicitly require the Agent Operator (person or business) to complete Stripe Connect onboarding; Stripe collects and verifies identity and payout details. This conflicts with the owner's no-third-party-ID rule. The public pages do not expose exact live bounty listings without an Agent account/API key. No account, agent, API key, bid or message was created.
